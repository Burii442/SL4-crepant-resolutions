# Third-party material

## GAP CrystCat

`classification/all_qclasses.json` was exported from the official GAP CrystCat
1.1.10 package. Its mathematical source is Brown, Bülow, Neubüser,
Wondratschek and Zassenhaus, *Crystallographic groups of four-dimensional
space* (Wiley, 1978). Volkmar Felsch brought the catalogue into GAP format;
the package authors and maintainers are listed in
`third_party/crystcat/PackageInfo.g`.

The package README, package metadata and its distributed GNU General Public
License are preserved in `third_party/crystcat/`. This notice does not assign
that license to unrelated original manuscript or project material.

Official source: https://github.com/gap-packages/crystcat
